# Recommendations

Written 5 Nov 2026 from the Mobbin research (`docs/research/`) and integrations research
(`docs/integrations/`, sources cited there). Items the research could not verify are flagged
there as **unverified**.

## 1. Product shape

- **One consumer app, two ways in.** Discover shows the same nights as **Events** (SeatGeek:
  poster cards by when) or **Clubs & lounges** (Resy: venue cards with bookable table tiers;
  "Notify me" when full). Event and venue pages borrow OpenTable's structure: sticky section
  tabs, icon fact rows, "Notes for tonight", and a **Music & vibe** block.
- **Tickets path:** tiers → one-sheet checkout → pay → QR in wallet.
- **Table path (the heart):** party & time → **floor plan** → **bottles toward the minimum**
  → guests → **review & commit (policy first)** → pay deposit → confirmed. Optional extras
  (occasion, requests) come after the commit, never before.
- **Guests join from WhatsApp** without installing anything: a web invite page, a phone code,
  accept or pay a share, and get a guest pass.
- **Promoter app** (separate tab set, same design system): tonight dashboard, live floor,
  reservations, approvals, door check-in that works offline, events, inventory, payouts, and
  WhatsApp broadcasts.

## 2. Tech

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| App type (v1) | **PWA** (installable web app) for consumer and door | No app-store download on metered data; opens straight from WhatsApp links; about 70% of users are on Android |
| UI | React 19 + Tailwind v4 + this Storybook | Small bundles (no UI kit), three brands from one set of tokens |
| Budgets | First load ≤ 150 KB; card images 15–25 KB WebP/AVIF; system body font | Data costs roughly $0.40–0.96 per GB |
| Data saver | On by default on 2G/3G; gradients instead of photos | Already built into `Img` |
| Offline | Signed QR passes cached on the device; the door app verifies offline and syncs later | Signal in a packed club is unreliable |
| Floor plans | Venue art as one SVG (10–20 KB) with vector table hit targets on top | Crisp at any zoom, almost free to ship, editable per venue |
| Native app | Later (Expo), only if PWA usage justifies it | Gains: zero-tap OTP, better iOS push, native wallets |

## 3. Auth: "login with WhatsApp"

There is **no consumer "Sign in with WhatsApp"** (no OAuth). What works:

1. The user enters a phone number (🇬🇭 +233 by default).
2. We send a code with a **WhatsApp authentication template** (copy-code button). **SMS is the
   fallback**, formatted for Android WebOTP autofill.
3. One-tap and zero-tap autofill exist only in native Android apps. On iOS 26+, the keyboard
   suggests the code from the notification.
4. Age: the user declares their date of birth and confirms 18+/21+. **Door staff check ID.** A
   Ghana Card check (Smile ID) is optional in v2. **Never store ID images** (NIA rules).

## 4. WhatsApp: can we push to it?

Yes, through the **Meta Cloud API directly**:

- **Utility templates** (confirmed, receipt, reminder, changed, cancelled/refund, ticket
  ready): about **$0.0046 each** for Ghana (Rest of Africa rate), roughly **$0.018 per
  booking**. Since 1 Oct 2026 these are charged even inside the 24-hour window.
- **Marketing templates** (event drops): about **$0.026 each**, **opt-in only**.
- **Guest invites** go as **`wa.me` share links sent from the host's own WhatsApp**. Guests
  haven't opted in to us yet, so we can't message them first.
- **WhatsApp Flows** can collect an RSVP or party size in-chat, but **payment must hand off to a
  Paystack link** (WhatsApp Pay isn't available in Ghana).
- **Blocking: start Meta Business Verification now.** Unverified businesses are capped at 250 new
  users a day.

## 5. Payments & deposits

- **Paystack Ghana** as the single primary rail: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, cards, Apple
  Pay. Flat **1.95%**. Payouts to MoMo cost ₵1. Hubtel is the v2 fallback.
- **Deposits are charged, not held.** Mobile money can't be pre-authorised, and card holds work
  only in South Africa. So we **charge the deposit now and refund it under a published policy**.
  That is why the policy block comes first on Review and repeats on the booking and the cancel
  sheet.
- **Ask counsel:** can we hold deposits and pay venues after the event (Payment Systems Act)? If
  not, split the payment to the venue's subaccount at checkout.

## 6. Bitcoin & bitchat

- **Not in v1.** Ghana's virtual-asset law (Act 1154) has passed, but **licensing hasn't
  opened**, and regulators have **banned public crypto advertising**. Even showing a "Pay with
  Bitcoin" button needs a legal opinion.
- **v2 option:** a feature-flagged **Lightning deposit** through a partner that settles to us in
  cedis (Bitnob is the most relevant local candidate). Refunds go back as a Lightning payment or
  to MoMo. The Pay screens include this as **Beta** so we can test the experience.
- **bitchat:** real (a Bluetooth-mesh / Nostr messenger) and it can redeem Cashu ecash tokens
  through an external wallet, but there's **no SDK**. Offline tokens aren't final until someone
  redeems them online ("first redeemer wins"), so they're unsafe for door payments.
  **Recommendation: don't integrate.** Signed offline QR passes solve the "no signal at the
  door" problem better.

## 7. Email

**Resend + React Email**, previewed as Storybook stories (**Emails**). Email is the record;
WhatsApp is the nudge. Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC from day one.
