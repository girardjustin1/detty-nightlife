# Detty Nightlife — Storybook drafts

Mobile-first booking for Accra nightlife: **event tickets, VIP tables, bottles toward a table
minimum, guests, and deposits** — plus a **promoter app** to run the night. Phase 1 is every
screen as a story. Nothing here talks to a server; all venues, artists, people and prices are
**fictional sample data**.

## How to look around

- **Toolbar → Brand** switches every screen between the three directions: **Detty** (loud,
  joyful), **Aurum** (luxe VIP) and **Vybe** (neon, music-first). See **Brand / Directions**.
- **Toolbar → Data saver** shows what the app looks like with photos off (the default on slow
  networks).
- Every important state has its own story: empty, loading, error, sold out, minimum not met…

## The core idea

Resy's **commit** model, applied to a club table: pick the table on the map, pre-order bottles
until the **minimum-spend meter** fills, add your squad, see the **policy first** (exact cedi
refund by exact time), pay a deposit by **mobile money**, and get a QR that works offline.
