# Detty Nightlife: Storybook drafts

<Hero />

A mobile-first booking app for Accra nightlife: **event tickets, VIP tables, bottles toward a table minimum, guests and
deposits**, plus a **promoter app** to run the night. This Storybook is phase 1: every screen as a story, before any
backend exists. Nothing here talks to a server, and every venue, artist, person and price is **fictional sample data**.

**Why it exists:** to agree on the product (the flows, the rules, the money and the look) cheaply, while changing it
is still a quick edit.

## Try the clickable prototype

Four ways to book the same night, side by side. Everything is prefilled, so just tap whatever glows. The question
they answer: **how much of a booking can live in WhatsApp, and what does an app add?**

<Prototypes />

On a phone, open the same links for the full-screen version. Locally, run `pnpm dev` and open http://localhost:5330.
The prototype also lives here under **Prototype / Click-through**.

## One night, end to end

The consumer app is built on Resy's **commit** model, applied to a club table: pick it on the map, fill the
**minimum-spend meter** with bottles, add your squad, see the **refund policy in cedis** before you pay, pay a deposit
by **mobile money**, and get a QR that works offline.

## How to look around

- **Toolbar → Brand** switches every screen between the directions. The default is **Detty Gold × Vybe type**,
  the current look. The others are **Detty Gold** (the blend from the first review, with Playfair) and the three
  originals: **Detty** (loud, joyful), **Aurum** (luxe VIP) and **Vybe** (neon, music-first).
- **Toolbar → Type** tries a different display font on any screen without changing the brand.
- **Toolbar → Data saver** shows the app with photos off, which is the default on slow networks.
- Every important state has its own story: empty, loading, error, sold out, minimum not met…
