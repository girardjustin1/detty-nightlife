# Detty Nightlife: Storybook drafts

Mobile-first booking for Accra nightlife: **event tickets, VIP tables, bottles toward a table
minimum, guests and deposits**, plus a **promoter app** to run the night. Phase 1 is every screen
as a story. Nothing here talks to a server, and all venues, artists, people and prices are
**fictional sample data**.

## How to look around

- **Toolbar → Brand** switches every screen between four directions: **Detty** (loud, joyful),
  **Aurum** (luxe VIP), **Vybe** (neon, music-first) and **Detty Gold**, the consolidated look
  from the first review. See **Brand / Directions** and **Brand / Evolution**.
- **Toolbar → Data saver** shows the app with photos off, which is the default on slow networks.
- Every important state has its own story: empty, loading, error, sold out, minimum not met…

## The core idea

Resy's **commit** model, applied to a club table:

1. Pick the table on the map.
2. Pre-order bottles until the **minimum-spend meter** fills.
3. Add your squad.
4. See the **policy first**: the exact refund in cedis, by the exact time.
5. Pay a deposit by **mobile money**.
6. Get a QR that works offline.
